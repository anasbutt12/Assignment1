About Bole Machinery

Bole Machinery designs and builds servo energy-saving injection moulding machines. Our machines use German-engineered central clamping, which spreads force evenly over the mould and gives high precision parts with less wear.

We work with factories making car parts, medical products, packaging and electronics. Our goal is simple: strong machines that use less power.
